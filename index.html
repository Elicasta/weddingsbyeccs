A Few Details

Questions before you choose.

Do you have any payment plans?

Yes, we do. We offer flexible payment plans from now through your big day, so your investment can be spread out over time.

Can we book photography without video?

Yes. Photography is the base collection. Film is a separate add-on and is completely optional.

Can our film coverage be shorter than our photography coverage?

Yes. Some couples want photography from preparations through the end of the reception while film begins later. We can position the film hours where they matter most.

Where do we make our final selections?

Your official Pixieset quote is the source of truth for your final photography collection, film add-ons, and investment. This proposal is designed to help you understand the options before you choose.

Can we customize the timeline?

Yes. These collections create a clean starting point. We plan the actual coverage around your wedding instead of forcing the wedding into a rigid template.
