A Few Details

Questions Before You Choose

Why 8, 10, and 12 hours?

Those windows cover the way most full wedding days actually unfold. Eight hours protects the core story, ten gives the day breathing room, and twelve is built for long timelines, multiple locations, or coverage through the final exit.

Do you record ceremony audio?

Yes. Clear vows and ceremony audio are treated as part of the story, not an afterthought. The exact documentary edits included depend on the collection.

Can we add rehearsal dinner or welcome party coverage?

Yes. Multi-day coverage is quoted separately so you only add it when the weekend actually needs it.

Can the collection be customized?

Yes. The three collections create a clean starting point. If your timeline needs something different, we can build around the day rather than forcing the day into a package.
